# Environment

`floor.glb` and `shell.glb` are generated by `tools/blender/environment.py` (own work, CC0).
glTF Y-up, metres, origin = room centre at floor level, floor top at y = 0, front (+Z) open.

- `floor.glb` (node `Floor`): titanium floor panels with chamfered seams, a polished centre disc
  with a hexagon inlay, LED lines, and flush docking rings at the five workstation spots
  (main (0, 0) r 2.4; bookshelf (-4.2, -3.4), lab (4.4, -3.4), file desk (-5.8, 1.2),
  writing desk (5.8, 1.2), r 1.3). Materials: Floor, FloorAlt, Seam, Pad, Metal, Graphite,
  GlowCyan, GlowPurple, GlowOrange.
- `shell.glb` (nodes Walls, Glass, Door, Holo, Racks, Pillars, Plants): curved back/side walls,
  4.5 m high. The back wall is at z = -6.95 with a panoramic window over x -7.5..7.5
  (glass y 0.5..3.95, empty space behind it); side walls at x = ±10.1 from z = -4.35 to z = +5.
  The door (closed) is on the left wall centred at z = 0.95; the hologram display is on the
  left wall at z = -2.6; the server racks are on the right wall at z = -1.15..-4.25. Pillars at (±8.75, z 4.45); plants at (±8.85, z -5.7), (-9.35, z 3.3), (9.35, z 1.0).
  Materials: Panel, Metal, Graphite, GlowCyan, GlowPurple, GlowOrange, Glass, Holo, Leaf, Pot, Seam.

Rebuild: `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/blender/environment.py` (Blender 5.2).
Check: `python3 -I tools/blender/inspect_glb.py assets/models/environment/floor.glb`; previews:
`Blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/blender/environment_preview.py -- OUT_DIR app close side back`.
